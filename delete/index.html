Delete your Battle Tees data

Battle Tees is made by Vexcom LLC. This page explains how to delete your account and data, and what happens when you do.

Option 1: from inside the app

  1. Open Battle Tees and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Start Over and confirm.

This erases everything on that phone (your profile, players, courses, rounds and stats) and replaces your cloud backup with an empty one. If you only want to leave the app, this is enough.

Option 2: ask us to delete everything on our server

Email privacy@vexcomllc.com with the subject Delete my Battle Tees data. Include the name and the email address you used in the app so we can find your records. We will confirm by reply once it is done, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, handicap index, email address and photo.
  • Your cloud backup (players, courses, round history, stats and personal challenges).
  • Your handicap record on our server.
  • Your entries in the usage log that carry your name or profile identifier.
  • Any pending sign-in or backup links tied to your email.

What stays

  • Scores and tracked shots in rounds you shared with other players stay on those players' phones and in their backups. They belong to the group, and we cannot reach into other people's devices.
  • A round you announced on the Community Battlefield stays visible to the friends who saved it, until they delete it.
  • Tee and green marks you added to an unmapped course stay in the shared course map. They contain no personal information.
  • If a copy of your data exists in a server backup file, we remove it from there too as part of the same request.
