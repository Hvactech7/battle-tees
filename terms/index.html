7. Yardages, course maps, conditions and safety

Yardages and maps are estimates. They come from:

  • your phone's GPS;
  • maps built from OpenStreetMap and other course data;
  • satellite imagery from Esri;
  • scorecard data from third parties;
  • marks made by players.

Any of these can be wrong or out of date: GPS drifts, greens move, courses are redesigned. "Plays-like" numbers for wind, temperature and elevation are estimates too. They use forecast data from the U.S. National Weather Service and elevation data from the U.S. Geological Survey.

When it matters, check the course's own markers, the pin and your own judgment.

Be safe on the course.

  • Don't use Battle Tees while driving a cart.
  • Look up from your phone before anyone swings, and watch for other golfers, balls, carts and hazards.
  • Follow the course's rules and its staff.
  • Golf involves risks, including being hit by a ball or club, falls, heat and lightning. You accept those risks when you play.
  • Battle Tees is not a safety tool, and nothing in it tells you a shot or path is safe.

Location. Battle Tees uses your phone's location only while you're using the app or playing a round. On Android, a notification stays on screen during a round so yardages keep working with the screen off. See the Privacy Policy for details.
