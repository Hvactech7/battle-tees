Nassau

The classic — front nine, back nine, overall

The basics

The most famous golf bet in America is three bets in one round: the front nine, the back nine, and the overall 18 — each its own match. A “$5 Nassau” means $5 on each.

It's match play — holes won, not strokes — played 1 v 1 or 2 v 2 best ball. A rough front nine can't touch the back-nine bet, so everyone grinds to the last putt.

The press

A side that's down can press: a brand-new bet at the same stake covering the remaining holes of that nine. Down 2 with four to play? Press, and there's a fourth bet running.

Battle Tees supports presses anytime you're down, classic 2-down automatics (every bet spawns a press the first time it falls exactly two down — cascades and all), or off. The app tracks every live bet so nobody's doing press math in the cart.

⚡How to actually win it

You're playing three matches, not one. Front, back, and overall are separate bets. Losing the front 4-down doesn't cost you anything extra — so the moment the front is gone, forget it entirely and start the back fresh. Players who carry a bad nine into the next one lose two bets instead of one.

Press when you're 2 down, not when you're angry. A press is a brand-new bet from that point to the end of the nine. Down two with seven to play is a genuine opportunity. Down two with two to play is just handing over money.

Accepting a press is usually correct. If they're pressing you, they're behind — and you're being offered a fresh bet while leading. Declining out of caution is how comfortable leads become nervous ones.

Watch the overall. It's the bet that quietly decides the day. A player who splits the front and back can still take the money by winning the eighteen-hole match.

🔥Why it's the oldest bet in golf

The Nassau dates to the Nassau Country Club around 1900, invented so club captains could lose a match without the score appearing in the newspaper as an embarrassment — the three-bet structure capped the damage at three points. A face-saving device that became the default wager of the entire sport.

It endures because it's perfectly paced. Two fresh starts a round means nobody is ever more than nine holes from redemption, and the overall bet keeps the whole day connected.

And the press is the best mechanic in golf gambling: a formal, structured way to say "double or nothing" that everyone at the club already understands.

💰The wager

A Nassau is quoted as one number — a "$5 Nassau" means $5 on the front, $5 on the back, and $5 on the overall. Nine units maximum on a clean sweep in a 2 v 2, before presses.

Presses are where the real money hides. Each one is a new bet running to the end of its nine, and with automatic presses turned on they cascade — a bad back nine can spawn three or four live bets at once. A $5 Nassau with heavy pressing can settle north of $50.

Mixed handicaps? The name

Your group, your point structure.Every group has its own house rules, and the app bends to them instead of the other way round. In Nassau that means the press rule: off entirely, manual (anyone down can press by tapping), or automatic (a press fires the first time a bet goes 2 down, and cascades from there). It also means 2-player singles or 2 v 2. Born at Nassau Country Club in Glen Cove, New York, around 1900 — invented, the story goes, so badly beaten members never lost worse than “3 points” in the posted results. The county — and the Bahamian capital — are both named for the same House of Orange-Nassau.
