Umbrella

Six points a hole — sweep them all for twelve

The basics

Umbrella is a 2-v-2 game where every hole has six points on offer across four categories: low ball 2, low team total 2, closest to pin 1, gross birdie 1.

Ties on a category mean nobody gets it. The birdie point is always gross — no strokes.

☂ The umbrella

Win all six points on one hole and it doubles to twelve. Dominance pays. Battle Tees counts your “umbrellas opened” in the standings.

Closest-to-pin is tapped every hole — any hole, not just par 3s — so short game matters everywhere.

⚡How to actually win it

Chase the sweep, but not stupidly. Taking all six points doubles the hole to twelve. That's the whole game — one swept hole is worth two ordinary ones. When your team already has low ball and low total locked up, spending a shot to steal the closest-to-pin is genuinely correct.

Closest to pin is a full point every par 3. That's four or five free points a round that most groups treat as an afterthought. Aim at the middle of the green and let the point come to you rather than short-siding yourself at a tucked flag.

The birdie point is exclusive — it doesn't stack. Know which points are still live before deciding how aggressive to be on your approach.

Low team total rewards the partner having a bad day. Two bogeys often beat a birdie and a double. Keep your partner in the hole even when their ball can't win it outright.

🔥Why the sweep is worth the wait

Umbrella gives every hole four separate things to fight over, so there's always something at stake even when the hole is otherwise decided. Lost the low ball? Low total is still live. Lost both? There's still a birdie and a closest-to-pin.

Then there's the sweep. Taking all six and watching the hole double to twelve is the loudest moment in the game — and the app counts them for you at the end, so you know exactly how many umbrellas you opened.

It's the thinking golfer's team game: four small battles per hole, and knowing which one to fight is the skill.

💰The wager

Six points a hole, twelve on a sweep — 108 points minimum across 18 holes and considerably more if anyone is sweeping.

The spread usually lands 20–45 points, so a quarter a point is a $5–12 round. Sweeps are where the variance lives: two of them in a round can decide the whole thing.

Mixed handicaps? Play it net.Umbrella takes strokes off the low man across all six points — so a stroke can win you low ball and low total on the same hole, which is four of the six points in one go. Battle Tees computes every player's course handicap from their index and the tees they actually played, then keeps gross and net side by side, live, on every phone — so the scratch sees their real number while everyone bets the one that decides the money.
